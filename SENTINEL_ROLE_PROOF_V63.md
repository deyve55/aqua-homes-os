## Truth and safety state

- This is a local role demonstration, not a production backend.
- The microphone activates only after the user taps Aqua.
- Audio buffers are not stored.
- The Android manifest intentionally omits the Internet permission.
- No API key, OpenAI call, customer record, payment, payroll, accounting write, or live export is
  present.
- All financial, project, draw, field, and Vault values are sample development data.
- Live Aqua intelligence remains behind the future approved HTTPS gateway.

## Interaction proof

1. Tap Aqua's `A`.
2. Grant microphone permission on first use.
3. Say, “Good morning, Aqua.”
4. Aqua replies, “Good morning, Davy. I’m online and ready. What would you like to handle first?”
5. Android TTS word-range callbacks trigger the voice light so pulse timing follows the actual
   spoken response.
6. Tap Aqua while she is speaking to stop her and return to listening.
7. Swipe the lower business deck; the upper app orbit synchronizes to the selected brief.
