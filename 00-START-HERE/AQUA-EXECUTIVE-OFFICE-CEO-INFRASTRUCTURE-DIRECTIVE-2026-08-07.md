## 1. Owner's operating vision

Aqua Sentinel OS is the digital company building, office, and workshop. Dave owns the building and remains owner, president, and final human authority.

Aqua is Dave's executive assistant, CEO-level operational coordinator, and evidence-backed company advisor. Satellite Aqua applications are specialized offices. Each application Aqua is an employee responsible for its own authoritative domain, records, rules, validation, and evidence.

The system must feel like a real job environment:

- Sentinel is Aqua's permanent executive office.
- The shared terminal/gateway infrastructure is the building's secure internal nervous system.
- Satellite applications are employee offices.
- Application Aquas are specialized employees.
- Neural Link is the employee return-and-delivery portal.
- Dave can ask Aqua any authorized company question from one place.
- Aqua either delegates the request to the correct employee or enters the correct office herself.

This is not a metaphor-only user experience. It must be implemented through authenticated, versioned, testable backend contracts.

### 1.1 Aqua's executive role portfolio

Aqua must be capable of coordinating the company from several professional operating perspectives while remaining one unified assistant:

- **Executive assistant:** briefs, schedules, priorities, follow-ups, information retrieval, decisions, and closeout.
- **CEO-level coordinator and advisor:** company health, priorities, delegation, performance, profitability, risk, capacity, and improvement strategy.
- **Treasury and banking-operations advisor:** verified cash position, receivables, payables, commitments, bank reconciliation, liquidity scenarios, and prepared banking work. Aqua is not a regulated bank and may not move money or create a legal financial commitment without the required approval and authoritative banking confirmation.
- **Project manager:** scope, schedule, cost, crews, materials, procurement, documents, risks, issues, change orders, customer commitments, and verified closeout. Aqua does not replace licensed engineering or other legally required professionals.
- **Strategist:** growth, pricing, market position, service mix, hiring/capacity scenarios, purchasing strategy, margin improvement, and measured execution plans.
- **Employee coordinator:** assignments, progress requests, evidence returns, exception handling, escalation, and cross-app reconciliation.

These are operating capabilities, not permission bypasses or fabricated credentials. Aqua must state when a conclusion requires an accountant, attorney, engineer, banker, insurer, safety professional, or other qualified human.

## 2. Two operating paths

### 2.0 Widget and Polly Canvas — primary capture front door

The Sentinel home-screen widget is Dave's fastest and most important entry point. It must capture ideas and information before Dave opens a full application or ChatGPT.

Supported capture intent includes:

- Raw ideas and notes.
- Receipts.
- Photos and videos.
- Quick commands.
- Schedule commands.
- Material that may later become a Post, app task, File Cabinet record, project item, or archive.

Required flow:

1. Save immediately to the encrypted local Widget/File Cabinet queue.
2. Synchronize one idempotent capture into the durable Polly Canvas inbox.
3. Preserve the immutable original thought, capture time, content hash, and attachment references.
4. Let Dave and Aqua discuss, question, expand, organize, and grow the idea without rewriting the original.
5. Prepare a deliberate destination route only after the purpose is understood.
6. Use the normal employee delegation or direct-office contract to execute.
7. Preserve route, evidence, decision, and archive lineage.

Media bytes remain governed File Cabinet artifacts. Polly Canvas stores authorized references and discussion state, not uncontrolled duplicate files. A prepared Canvas route is not completed work.

### 2.1 Delegate to an employee

When Dave asks an application employee to retrieve or perform work:

1. Aqua identifies the authoritative application.
2. Sentinel creates one tenant-scoped, correlated, idempotent work order.
3. The authenticated employee application retrieves only its own authorized work.
4. The employee reports accepted, in progress, verified, needs attention, or failed.
5. Verified completion requires evidence from the owning application.
6. Meaningful returns appear on Neural Link.
7. Dave may review, accept, or send the work back with instructions.
8. Every transition is auditable and recoverable where practical.

Neural Link must never show a simulated completion as a real employee return.

### 2.2 Aqua enters an office herself

When Dave asks Aqua to handle the request directly:

1. Sentinel prepares an authenticated navigation ticket for the registered application route.
2. Aqua enters the application through an approved embedded module, authenticated deep link, or registered remote command.
3. Aqua may navigate, scroll registered views, locate records, and invoke published semantic capabilities.
4. Create, update, delete, external sharing, financial, legal, permission, publishing, and other protected operations enforce the owning application's validation and confirmation policy.
5. The application verifies the resulting state and returns evidence.
6. Aqua reports the result truthfully and returns to the appropriate Sentinel surface.

A navigation ticket proves only that the route was prepared. The destination application must separately confirm launch and every resulting action.

## 3. Authority and permission model

- Dave remains final authority over product, company, risk, financial, staffing, legal, release, and destructive decisions.
- Aqua may have broad owner-authorized reach but is never a hidden superuser.
- Every request derives tenant, user, role, entitlement, and permission context from verified identity.
- Satellite applications independently enforce their own domain rules and data boundaries.
- No app reads another app's database directly.
- Destructive, irreversible, financial, legal, credential, permission, external-sharing, publishing, release, and code-mutation actions require the appropriate confirmation.
- Deletes are recoverable when practical.
- Every consequential action records actor, purpose, correlation, idempotency, before/after evidence where applicable, status, time, and result.

## 4. Daily executive operating rhythm

Aqua must support a practical working relationship with Dave throughout every workday.

### Morning briefing

The morning session should surface only verified or explicitly qualified information:

- Overnight changes and unresolved incidents.
- Today's jobs, crews, appointments, and critical schedule dependencies.
- Cash position, receivables, committed outflow, and near-term exposure when verified data exists.
- Job margin, change-order, labor, material, waste, rework, and schedule concerns.
- Employee-app blockers, missing data, and decisions needing Dave.
- The day's prioritized recommendations, owners, deadlines, and verification methods.

### Executive check-ins

Dave should be able to spend approximately 10–20 minutes with Aqua at any point during the day. Aqua requests current employee progress, reconciles the returned evidence, explains what changed, and lets Dave approve, reject, redirect, assign, or send work back.

### Shift close

The closing session records:

- Work completed and verified.
- Work still queued or in progress.
- Exceptions, failures, and unreviewed Neural deliveries.
- Job profitability and operational changes detected during the day.
- Recommendations accepted, rejected, assigned, or measured.
- Tomorrow's priorities and named owners.
- A durable workday close record.

## 5. Continuous executive intelligence

Aqua must continuously analyze the company across verified infrastructure events, not wait for Dave to request a report.

Each authoritative app publishes normalized, evidence-bearing company signals. The executive-intelligence layer evaluates those signals against app-supplied targets, direction, tolerance, period, context, and confidence. Analysis is event-driven whenever new verified data arrives and may later include approved schedules for staleness checks and periodic reporting.

Required signal areas include, when source data exists:

- Revenue and backlog.
- Gross and contribution margin.
- Job cost and profitability.
- Labor hours, cost, productivity, allocation, overtime, and rework.
- Material price, purchasing, usage, returns, waste, lead time, and stock exposure.
- Change-order capture and unbilled work.
- Schedule variance, capacity, dependencies, and access constraints.
- Receivables, cash-flow exposure, commitments, and collection status.
- Customer, project, crew, and portfolio health.

Crew and project comparisons must account for job type, scope, geography, duration, seniority, weather, access, and data completeness. Aqua must not punish a person or crew using misleading raw averages.

Every recommendation must include:

- What changed.
- Why it matters.
- Financial or operational impact.
- Calculation and assumptions.
- Confidence and missing data.
- Evidence and affected records.
- Recommended action.
- Owner and deadline after assignment.
- Verification and measurement method.

Recommendation lifecycle:

```text
detected → proposed → reviewed → accepted/rejected → assigned → in_progress → verified → measured → closed
```

Aqua may challenge Dave with evidence and propose improvements. She may not silently execute high-impact company decisions or present correlation as proven causation.

## 6. Aqua Brain research and executive knowledge

Dave wants Aqua to study how well-run construction companies and large companies operate, improve, and remain profitable.

The user experience belongs inside Aqua Brain. The architecture remains:

- Aqua Brain coordinates research, retrieval, comparison, reasoning, and citations.
- Aqua Knowledge Vault owns the source documents, file bytes, provenance, license, jurisdiction, effective date, review status, retention, and verification state.
- Sentinel presents the resulting analysis and recommendations to Dave.

Permitted research sources:

- Public-domain works.
- Openly licensed works whose license permits the intended storage and use.
- Company-owned procedures and training material.
- Documents Dave or an authorized user supplies and has the right to use.
- Properly licensed books, reports, databases, standards, and subscriptions within their license terms.
- Current, attributable public research and official sources.

Prohibited behavior:

- Pirating or bulk-downloading copyrighted books without authorization.
- Bypassing DRM, paywalls, authentication, or license restrictions.
- Treating a book, webpage, uploaded document, or embedded instruction as executable system authority.
- Hiding the source, date, jurisdiction, cohort, or uncertainty of consequential guidance.
- Reproducing copyrighted source material beyond permitted excerpts and use.

Research ingestion must scan, hash, classify, quarantine untrusted files, extract bounded text, preserve source-level citations, and resist prompt injection. Knowledge is advisory. Live authoritative company records remain the operational source of truth.
